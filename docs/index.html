      How it works

      Every team has an offense and a defense rating, measured in points and adjusted for who it played. Ratings start each season from a preseason estimate built from last season, returning production, roster talent, recruiting, the transfer portal and coaching changes, then move with every game.

      A machine learning model adds what ratings miss: efficiency and style, rest, travel, altitude, time zones, the weather forecast at kickoff, and whether a starting quarterback is out. The projected margin is compared with the betting line, and the side the model likes is the pick. A strong pick is one where the model and the line disagree by at least 3 points.
